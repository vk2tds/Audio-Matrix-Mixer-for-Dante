A grid of buttons for your saved mixer snapshots, and live VU meter tiles. Press a snapshot button to apply it — unlike the routing Panel, pressing again does not reverse it (v1). A VU tile shows one channel, or two side by side as a stereo pair; it renders as a vertical meter when the button is square or taller than wide, and horizontal when it's wider than tall. These buttons only ever touch mixer volumes/mutes — never Dante routing. In edit mode, drag a button onto an empty cell to move it.
