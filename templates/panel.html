A grid of buttons for your saved routing presets. Press a button to apply its preset; press it again to reverse it (unsubscribes what it added, restores what it removed, where possible). Multiple buttons can be lit at once. A button glows at half intensity when its preset's rules already hold true on the live matrix, even if you didn't press it. Label buttons are just static text, for grouping. A read-only button can't be pressed by clicking, but still lights up automatically when its preset's rules match live. A device-present button lights up whenever a chosen Dante device is online — it's never clickable. In edit mode, drag a button onto an empty cell to move it. Press state, rule-matching and device presence are all live — layout (position, size, text, icon, colour) is saved to disk.
